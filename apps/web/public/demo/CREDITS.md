# Demo product images — credits and licences

All products, sellers and reviews in the demo catalog are **fictional**. The images are placeholders that make the storefront look populated; replace them with each seller's own product photography before any real launch.

- **34 photographs** from Wikimedia Commons, used under the licence shown for each. Every file was cropped to a square and resized (an adaptation). CC BY-SA images stay under CC BY-SA.
